Assistant on documents and contracts

The answer to a question about a contract is often a single clause, buried in a file nobody remembers. This assistant answers from the uploaded documents and, for every sentence, shows the passage it comes from.

Answers on contracts and procedures, with citations from the documents, for a fictitious mechanical SME with about 50 employees.

How it works

Every document is split into passages with a stable id: article, section or clause. The answer can only rest on those passages, and says which ones it used, quoting them word for word.

Documents split into passages

  • Client contract
  • Steel agreement
  • Procedure PQ-07
  • CNC service

Answer with citations

Two checks

  • Quoted text found in the passage
  • A person reads the passage and confirms

The first check is automatic. The second stays with whoever uses the answer: the passage is one click away.

Possible benefits

When every answer brings its passage along, checking it means reading a few lines instead of a whole contract. Flip the switch and compare the two situations.

  • Before Every question about a contract means rereading it to find the clause.

    After Every question about a contract gets an answer with the clause it refers to.

  • Before Every answer relies on a document to reopen to find the passage.

    After Every answer cites the point in the document, so whoever checks it finds it right away.

  • Before Every internal procedure has to be searched for across folders and versions.

    After Every internal procedure can be queried from one place, on the uploaded documents.

Use case with fictitious data. The benefits described are possible, not results achieved with a client.
