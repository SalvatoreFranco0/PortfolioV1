Compliance assistant

A doubt about privacy, cookies or AI often has its answer in a single paragraph of a long regulation. This assistant answers from the official texts and, for every sentence, shows the passage it comes from.

Answers on the GDPR, the AI Act and the Garante's cookie guidelines, with sources, for a fictitious mechanical SME with about 50 employees. Support, not legal advice.

How it works

The official texts are split by article and paragraph, each with a stable id. The answer can only rest on those passages, quotes them word for word and leaves the final judgement to a person.

Answer with citations

Two checks

  • Quoted text found in the article
  • A person reads the article and decides on the actual case

The first check is automatic. The second stays with whoever uses the answer, with a professional where needed: the article is one click away.

Possible benefits

When every answer brings its article along, checking it means reading a paragraph instead of a whole regulation. Flip the switch and compare the two situations.
