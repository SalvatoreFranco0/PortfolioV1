Privacy

This site does not use cookies, statistics or services that send your data to other companies. Here is what happens to your data, in plain words.

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Information under Articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679).

Who is responsible

Salvatore Franco, private individual, Venegono Inferiore (VA), Italy. Email: salvatorefranco@outlook.it.

I am not a company and have no VAT number: this site presents my skills and does not sell services. There is no data protection officer, because none is required.

When you visit the site

Fonts and scripts are served by the site itself. There are no cookies, statistics, advertising, embedded videos, maps or social buttons. The use cases run entirely in your browser: what you click or move is not sent anywhere and is not saved.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). To deliver the pages and for security, GitHub records the IP address of every visitor and handles it on its own behalf, under its privacy statement. I have no access to these records.

What stays on your device

Only if you click the language or theme switch, the browser remembers your choice in its local storage. Nothing is written before the click.

  • lang: the language you chose, Italian or English.
  • theme: light or dark mode.

These values never leave your device, do not identify you and are not used to track you. They stay until you delete them from your browser settings. They are technical tools, so no consent banner is needed.

If you write to me

If you write by email or on LinkedIn, I use your name, your address and what you write only to reply and to continue the conversation you started.

The legal basis is your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) or my legitimate interest in replying to whoever contacts me (Article 6(1)(f)). Writing to me is optional; without your details I cannot reply.

If I contact you

I may write to people who work in companies of my sector to introduce my work. In that case I use your name, role, company and work contact details, taken from your company's website, your public LinkedIn profile or a meeting in person.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a first professional contact (Article 6(1)(f)): making my work known to companies that may need it. I only use generic company addresses or non-promotional channels, one message at a time, never mass or automated sending.

You can object at any time, even by just replying "no": I will not contact you again.

How long I keep data

  • Messages and contacts with no follow-up: 24 months from the last exchange, then deleted.
  • If a collaboration starts: the documents needed for legal obligations, for the time the law requires (usually 10 years).
  • If you ask not to be contacted: I delete your details at once and keep only your address on an exclusion list, so as not to write to you again.

Who else handles the data

I do not sell or pass on your data. They reach only the services I use:

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): site hosting.
  • Microsoft (Outlook, United States): my email inbox.
  • LinkedIn (LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company): messages exchanged on LinkedIn.

Some data may therefore go to the United States. These companies state that they adhere to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, covered by the European Commission adequacy decision of 10 July 2023, and use the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission. No decision about you is taken automatically and there is no profiling.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to receive it in a portable format, and you can object to its use (Articles 15-21 GDPR). Write to salvatorefranco@outlook.it: I reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Notes

The use cases on this site use fictitious data. The compliance assistant is support for reading the texts, not legal advice. If something on the site changes, for example new services, this page is updated first.
